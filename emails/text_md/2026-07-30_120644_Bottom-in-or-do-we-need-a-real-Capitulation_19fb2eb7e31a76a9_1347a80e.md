 Many people feel there is no Bitcoin bottom until we see real capitulation. On-chain data still agrees: the bottom has not been confirmed.

 That capitulation is the blue shaded area in the chart. 

 Yet a large group of analysts (working on the list) are already treating it as bottomed.

 In a normal BTC bottom cycle we get a big deleveraging and there is not that much leverage in the system today. Maybe this time is also different.
