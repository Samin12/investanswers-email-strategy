 Here is the mapping to my IA13 - we have a gap w Chinese AI Solution Providers. Thoughts welcome below. 

 (i) Own the AI Infrastructure bottlenecks Best fit from IA13: Tesla (TSLA) — only real match. Megapack energy storage + power solutions directly address “time to power” and energy storage for data centers. Optimus also gives a labor-bottleneck angle that almost nothing else in the list has.

 (ii) Own the compute manufacturing ecosystem Strongest cluster in the entire IA13 list. 

 NVIDIA (NVDA) — dominant GPU / CUDA

 Astera Labs (ALAB) — connectivity / retimers critical for scale-out AI clusters

 Broadcom (AVGO) — networking ASICs + custom silicon

 Marvell (MRVL) — high-speed networking / storage controllers

 AMD (AMD) — GPU + CPU alternative compute

 Micron (MU) — HBM / high-bandwidth memory (one of the tightest bottlenecks)

 ASML (ASML) — EUV lithography (the ultimate manufacturing chokepoint)

 TSMC (TSM) — leading-edge foundry capacity

 (iii) Own leading Chinese AI solution providers I have a small position in BABA but that is it. Need to look again. 

 (iv) Own Energy Security assets important to AI Infrastructure Tesla (TSLA) — Megapack is the purest large-scale energy-storage play among the 13 and is already being used for AI data-center power.

 (v) Own hyperscalers that can drive scale benefits and attractive ROI on AI capex Google (GOOGL) — explicitly named in the thesis and present in your list.

 Tesla (TSLA) — massive internal AI compute build-out (+ inference) 

 xAI & SpaceX — Top 5 hyperscaler
