 TLDR Investing Tips Volatility is a two-way street. The stocks that deliver the most explosive multi-year returns are usually the same ones that inflict the most brutal drawdowns.

 Summer is doing its usual thing. Folks at the beach, vol down, risk appetite fades, speculative names get punished first and hardest.

 Nothing here looks like a broad market collapse. It’s selective pain in the highest-flying disruption names while the bigger, higher-quality AI/tech compounders are just doing a normal mean reversion.

 Getting to be a good time to shop - hence the cash harvesting i urged a few months back for SUMMER SUX

 The Details The above image is my Summer Sux Distance from ATH Model. 

 This list is a perfect snapshot of mean reversion in action. The names that got crushed the hardest (Eos, Oklo, Navitas, Oracle, IonQ, Planet Labs, etc.) are almost all pure “disruption” stories — speculative, high-beta, narrative-driven stocks that went parabolic and are now giving a huge chunk of it back. Down 50-80% from their peaks. Classic what i call “what goes up fast comes down fast.”

 Meanwhile the more established Mag7 did not escape either. 

 Some Core IA13 names ie AI infrastructure names (NVDA, AMD, TSMC, ASML, Broadcom, etc.) are only 10-25% off their highs. They’re holding up way better. Some others not eg ALAB MU PLTR TSLA SPCX etc. Quick note on Tesla... it is down less than many other names like PLTR, SPCX, MRVL, ALAB, ORCL etc etc

 I warned before summer it was time to hoard cash.... as SUMMER SUX... and here we are. Typical. 

 Average across the whole list: –42.4% from all-time highs. That’s a solid correction.
