 Coldcard one of the most popular BTC hard wallets just discovered it had a serious firmware bug dating back to 2021. When the device created your recovery seed phrase, it wasn’t using strong enough randomness of the “secret words,” which were weaker and more guessable than they should have been.

 Attackers figured this out using AI (allegedly) and yesterday quickly drained hundreds of wallets. Rem as AI gets better expect more and more of this to happen. 

 Estimates range from $38 million (≈594 BTC from 500 wallets) up to as high as ~$70 million depending on the count. Most of the theft happened in less than an hour.

 Coinkite (the maker) released emergency fixed firmware the same day:

 Mk3 → version 4.2.0 or higher

 Mk4 / Mk5 → 5.6.0 or higher

 Q → 1.5.0Q or higher

 IMPORTANT PART Just updating the firmware does not fix an already-created weak seed. You have to generate a brand-new seed on the fixed firmware and move your coins to the new wallet.

 This is why sentiment around self-custody and cold wallets took a big hit — people are scared that even “air-gapped” hardware can still have hidden flaws. 

 I never had such a wallet but as you know I was always very fearful of self custody going way back to 2020 once value of BTC started to really increase.
