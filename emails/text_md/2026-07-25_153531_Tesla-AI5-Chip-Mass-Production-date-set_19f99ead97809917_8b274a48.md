 Samsung will build a large share of them at its new Texas factory (on their advanced 2nm process), and TSMC will make a bunch also in Taiwan. Tesla signed a $15.5 billion deal with Samsung last year. Elon thanked Samsung, TSMC, and Micron for pouring tens of billions into U.S. chip capacity to support Tesla Cybercab and Optimus robots.

 Cybercab runs on AI4 hardware and Elon has said AI4 is already enough to reach “much better than human” safety for unsupervised FSD. 

 AI5 has 5× more compute of a dual-AI4 system - AI4 is enough for basic Optimus roles but AI5 greatly enhance optimus capability. 

 For data centers in space AI5 (and especially AI6) will be usable for the early, lower-scale versions of this (“low GW/year” level).
