 The original Big Mac Index (created by The Economist) was designed for cross-country purchasing-power parity. The same logic applied over time inside one country is even more powerful for measuring the silent erosion of a currency as the cost of a big mac in a location is a function of cost of labor, rent, real estate, transport, meat etc. 

 BigMac is 27% more expensive since 2020 and if paying in BTC you now get 8 times more BigMacs.
