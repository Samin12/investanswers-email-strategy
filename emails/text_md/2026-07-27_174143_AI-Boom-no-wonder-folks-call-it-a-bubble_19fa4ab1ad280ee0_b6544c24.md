 Literally trillions going into AI. It has everyone nervous. 

 A bubble requires one core condition: capital is chasing claims that exceed the actual demand for the underlying thing. Railways, canals, and the 1990s internet all eventually ran into physical or practical ceilings. You only need so many tracks between two cities. But with Intelligence it has no limit as the demand for intelligence is infinite IMHO

 In addition, bubbles do not have bottlenecks, AI now has 4 bottlenecks

 1) power 

 2) chips 

 3) memory and 

 4) connectivity

 In just the last five months, approximately $1 trillion has been committed to AI infrastructure. According to Morgan Stanley, global AI capital expenditure (capex) is poised to see a 69% increase over the previous year to hit $740 billion in 2026 alone.
