 Goldman note on the recent leverage unwind and forced selling and the AI stocks in general.

 A lot of forced selling dislocation happened this month. If you have longer duration capital and can withstand ST vol, there are very attractive opportunities now as AI fundamentals are about to improve dramatically over the next 6–9 months

 The valuations for the HBM memory names are going to look silly in the coming year ie they (MU, SK Hynix, Samsung) are super cheap now!!! 

 Asia (especially Korea) under pressure, with SK Hynix delivering record numbers that still missed lofty consensus and accelerated the selloff.

 The move being primarily a deleveraging / de-grossing event among hedge funds, multi-strats, and quants (not a macro story).

 Heavy positioning unwind in Korea and Taiwan.
