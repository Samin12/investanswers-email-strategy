 Micron is still in a structural shortage, not a normal cycle. They’re meeting less than half of data-center demand right now. Hyperscalers and AI chip makers are so desperate for memory that they barely care about price, and they’re locking in multi-year deals out to 2030. Eg Elon did this for Tesla. Micron is also the only U.S. producer of these chips, which adds a policy tailwind.

 Prices are still rising hard (DRAM up ~20% this quarter, more next quarter; HBM assumptions raised). Capacity is being shifted from NAND into DRAM, stretching the shortage further. China’s CXMT is growing but remains a small player and doesn’t fix the global undersupply through at least mid-2028.

 The trade will end someday—but based on what management and the numbers show today, we’re not there yet. Still bullish on Micron. PT $1500 to $1600.
