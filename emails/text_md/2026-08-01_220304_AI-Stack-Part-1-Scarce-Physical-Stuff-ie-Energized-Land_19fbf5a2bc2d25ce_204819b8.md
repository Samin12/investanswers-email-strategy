 Bottom part of the stack: Land + Power + Shell is where the most reliable profits come from. It is all tied to physical scarcity. Think of it as Energized land and power - think Bitcoin miners. 

 Right now, energized land and power are very hard to build, take years, require huge capital, and are genuinely constrained. Eg a 50GW datacenter now costs between $50BN and $60BN if you can even get the approval to do it. 

 Demand for AI compute is exploding faster than supply can keep up, so that energized land will get more and more valuable over time.
