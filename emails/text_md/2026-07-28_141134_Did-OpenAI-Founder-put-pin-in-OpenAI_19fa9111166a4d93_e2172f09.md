 Nvidia is investing in Ilya Sutskever’s new secret AI lab, Safe Superintelligence (SSI)

 The ex-OpenAI chief scientist’s startup gets better access to Nvidia chips. Nvidia gets another high-profile customer and strengthens its position in the AI boom.

 The real interesting parts are 1) SSI ditching Google's TPUs for Nvidia's Vera Rubin GPUs. The AI infrastructure war just got a lot more interesting.

 2) Nvidia says it was given rare access to SSI’s closely guarded research and saw enough to make a massive commitment.

 3) SSI says this will 10x their compute in the next 12 months.

 4) Sutskever argued that the era of simply scaling LLMs is coming to an end. SSI is building something fundamentally different from today’s AI Frontier Lab architectures. This could hurt OpenAI, Anthropic etc if they can reinvent the model.
