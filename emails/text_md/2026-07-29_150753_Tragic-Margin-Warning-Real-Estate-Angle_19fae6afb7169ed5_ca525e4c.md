 As I have said for years in here... a key rule - I never use more than 20% of my margin just in case we hit a blackswan I can easily survive. 

 This story just hit. High Real Estate forces S Koreans to Gamble Young Koreans priced out of Seoul apartments turned the stock market into their only shot at a home — and got wiped out.

 Workers in their 20s and 30s could not afford housing, so they treated KOSPI like a housing trade aka casino. They borrowed at 3x margin, piled savings into 2x semiconductor Leveraged ETFs which are the kiss of death anyhow - so at 6xplus leverage thought it was a no lose. In fact, they were so confident they saw these short-term leveraged bets as long-term wealth building.

 When prices fell of late, brokers auto-liquidated 360,000 accounts. 62% of those destroyed were under 35. Margin loans had hit a record 38.63 trillion won; forced selling then made the drop worse, turning a normal decline into a cascade. Cascading liquidations are another kiss of death. 

 Retail losses on borrowed money hit 2.15 trillion won in a single month. One office worker lost 18 million won of his housing savings. The government even opened a debt crisis hotline because the pain spilled into mental health.

 KOSPI will recover. The people who bet money they could not afford to lose will not. 

 BEWARE OF MARGIN - the double edged sword
