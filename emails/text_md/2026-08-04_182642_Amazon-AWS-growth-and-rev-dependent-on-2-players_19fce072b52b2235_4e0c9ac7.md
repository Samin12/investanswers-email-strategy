 This was an interesting find, Amazon’s cloud division (AWS) is up huge on AI but according to Barclays analysts, the reality looks very concentrated:

 By 2028, Amazon’s AI-related cloud revenue is expected to hit about $75 billion.

 Roughly 73–74% of that money is projected to come from just two customers: Anthropic and OpenAI. Neither OpenAI or Anthropic make a profit. 

 I estimate their ability to charge high prices will diminish with opensource models like DeepSeek and Kimi K3 as well as Grok.
