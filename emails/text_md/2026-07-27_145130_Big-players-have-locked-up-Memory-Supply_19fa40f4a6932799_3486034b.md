 The biggest players are locking up supply with multi-year exclusive deals. The AI chip pipeline is getting tighter and more controlled by a few major players. If you snooze, you lose. 

 The big deals so far Tesla locked up their Micron memory supply. 

 SK Hynix signed a mega-deal with Nvidia

 Samsung and Broadcom just locked in a massive $200 billion, 5-year partnership.

 They’re bundling three key things: High-speed memory (especially the advanced HBM chips AI systems need)

 Actual chip manufacturing

 Packaging (putting the pieces together)
