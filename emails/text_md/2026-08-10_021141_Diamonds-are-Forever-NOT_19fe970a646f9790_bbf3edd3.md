 Lab-grown diamonds (made in factories, not mined) are now chemically identical to the real thing, and technology has made them ridiculously cheap to produce.

 A 1-carat lab diamond that cost ~$3,400 in 2020 is now around $500. Natural diamonds have also fallen hard (over 50% in real terms), even though mining companies cut production trying to prop up prices.

 Most engagement rings are already lab-grown. Huge tennis bracelets that used to cost a fortune can now be bought for the price of a used watch.

 Just like cultured pearls destroyed the exclusivity of natural pearls a century ago, lab diamonds are turning diamonds from a status symbol into something that increasingly looks tacky and common. The old “diamonds are forever” or rare or special story is DONE
