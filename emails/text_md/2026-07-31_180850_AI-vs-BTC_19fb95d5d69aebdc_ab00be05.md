 Saying for a long time now that AI is sucking the oxygen out of all the rooms 

 Well now Saylor is saying it also 

 AI Spending Is Pulling Capital Away From Bitcoin, Saylor Says 

 Saylor called the AI build-out the biggest of five headwinds currently weighing on the cryptocurrency.

 “There’s an AI capital expansion, $1 trillion or more of capital that are flowing into the data center build-out, the AI center build-outs with the SpaceX, the Google, the Meta, the Anthropic, the OpenAI.” Saylor said. “That’s created a capital suction in the equity capital markets and also an attraction in the private credit markets where massive amounts of credit capital has been flowing. That’s a headwind.”
