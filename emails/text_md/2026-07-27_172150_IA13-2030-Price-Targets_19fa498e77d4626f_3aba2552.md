 Hey Team

 Here is my current model for IA13 Price Targets. The summer price action sucks now across the board, and this is not financial advice but this is the summary of my models of where I believe these names could go. Always be prudent eg take bear target as your low. The bull targets are hard to reach and require extreme execution. As prices fall the future CAGR rises. 

 MY PLAN To continue to take my time this summer, we are not out of the woods, so my deployment will happen gradually as items hit my traps. eg I am a buyer of MU at $700 etc. 

 These are all high flying stocks with extreme volatility. Bear that in mind. AI has a bright future but rem the world will change radically over the next 3.5 years and who knows what it will look like in 2030 - perhaps the Chinese will take over all things AI by then. We shall see. 

 Have an allocation but do not go all in. Also you dont need to own them all. if I had to pick 4 it would be NVDA MU TSLA MRVL. 

 Some notes by asset: 

 NVDA Could lose share to custom AI chips and get valued more like a normal company (around 20x earnings) instead of a high-flyer.

 ALAB Smaller company, so bigger price swings. Needs to keep dominating the high-speed connections that link AI racks together.

 AVGO Must keep winning big custom-chip deals with the cloud giants and successfully shift to new optical tech.

 MRVL Success rides on maintaining leadership in Photonics, custom AI chips, next-gen optical chips, and memory expansion tech. Needs to keep winning those.

 AMD Needs real share gains in AI accelerators plus strong CPU growth from new AI workloads. Execution is everything.

 MU Still a memory company at heart. Needs long-term HBM contracts and pricing power to stop the old boom-bust cycle.

 TSLA Biggest wild card. Value now depends more on Robotaxi and Optimus working at scale than on cars. Execution risk is high. Terafab is also key to future 

 GOOGL More mature and defensive. Upside is real but smaller than pure AI plays; relies on Cloud and AI features actually making money.

 xAI/SpaceX Valuation swings hard with AI progress and rocket launch success. Starlink cash helps, but AI results drive the big upside. Space Based Datacenters even more upside. But this will take time to scale... so i expect big numbers post 2030. 

 PLTR Needs commercial growth to stay very high. Getting to $1T requires years of 40%+ growth continuing.

 Anduril Private defense AI play. Valuation jumps around. We do not know what it will launch at IPO. Big upside only if it wins a major share of future autonomous weapons spending. Lots of competition entering. 

 ASML Almost every advanced AI chip needs its machines. Risk is mainly timing and execution on the next-gen High-NA tools.

 TSM The go-to factory for AI chips. Biggest risk is geopolitics; demand itself looks very solid. Terafab if they pull it off could eat some share.
