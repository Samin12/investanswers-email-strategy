 By 2030, it is projected that inference will surpass training as the dominant workload in AI data centers, representing more than half of all AI compute and roughly 30% to 40% of total data-center demand. Its forecast shows global data-center demand rising from 82.3 GW in 2025 to 219.0 GW by 2030, with AI inference growing from 20.9 GW to 93.3 GW and AI training growing from 23.1 GW to 62.2 GW.

 Those that can furnish this near 100 GW of Inference Compute will make a killing. 

 Note 2030 is only 3.5 years away.
