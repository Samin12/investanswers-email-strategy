 The Internet made a lot of people a lot of money — roughly $15 trillion in new wealth. I lived and traded thru it. 

 AI is on track to create several times more than that, closer to 7x the scale. Be part of it. 

 The internet created roughly $15 trillion in company value over the last 30 years by making the cost of copying and sending information almost free. Once something was digital, the next copy cost almost nothing to produce or deliver worldwide.

 AI is going after something far bigger. Intelligence. Jensen Huang said AI was going to unlock a $100 trillion opportunity by driving the cost of intelligence and digital labor toward zero.
