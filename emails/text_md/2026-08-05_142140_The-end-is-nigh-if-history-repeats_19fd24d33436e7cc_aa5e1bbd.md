 The futures market is currently offering almost no reward for taking Bitcoin risk relative to risk-free Treasuries (ie xero yield from holding Bitcoin Futures). The last time this happened for so long, it marked the end of a major bear market.

 Only one other stretch on record has run this long: August 2022 into January 2023. It ended at the cycle low. We are 3 days away from that historic low.
