 Another NVDA / Memory Deal - that they say is worth over $500 billion. SK Telecom is building a giant AI data center (they call it an “AI Factory”) in Korea. It will use 2 gigawatts of power — that’s an enormous amount of electricity, enough to run a small city — and it will be packed with NVIDIA’s newest AI chips (called Vera Rubin DSX). SK Hynix will work closely with NVIDIA to design the next generation of ultra-fast memory chips that AI computers desperately need

 It seems it is all about securing supply at all costs. We seem to be going from bottleneck to bottleneck.
