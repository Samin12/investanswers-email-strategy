 NVDA knows if there is no power they cannot sell any chips. 

 Elon predicted by end of 2026 some chips would be dark ie powerless. 

 The “rush for power” is yet another AI bottleneck (on top of memory, transformers, connectors/photonics etc). After years where the constraint was chips (especially Nvidia GPUs), the new limiting factor has shifted hard to electricity — specifically, secure, firm, grid-connected power at the scale needed for modern AI data centers.
