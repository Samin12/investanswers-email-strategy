 Electricity is the physical substrate of modern wealth. The chart shows a very tight relationship ie higher electricity consumption per capita tracks higher GDP per capita almost linearly on a log-log scale. 

 There are essentially no rich countries that use little electricity. That is not a coincidence.

 In the age of AGI this is about to go into high gear. Electricity = Intelligence.
