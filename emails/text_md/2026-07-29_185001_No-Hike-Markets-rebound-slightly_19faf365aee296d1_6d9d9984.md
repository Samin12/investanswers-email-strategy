 The Fed held interest rates steady for the 5th meeting in a row.

 Kev Warsh said the U.S. economy is holding up surprisingly well, but inflation is still running too high above their 2% goal. Silly goal i know. They gave zero hints about future rate cuts or hikes — just the facts. They also made clear there’s no “soft” target; 2% is the only target.

 Bond yields have risen a lot since the last meeting, and the Fed noted that markets are finally focusing more on the actual economy instead of trying to second-guess the Fed. This is good news and this was Kevins plan all along.
