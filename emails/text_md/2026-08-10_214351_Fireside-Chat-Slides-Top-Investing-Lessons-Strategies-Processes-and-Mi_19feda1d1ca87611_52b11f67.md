 Hey Team

 Here are slides from the Fireside Chat. 

 It is basically a masterclass from real people in the community who’ve actually made investing work. Instead of theory or guru talk, you get their unfiltered definitions of success, how they study and review every day, the exact moments they almost quit (and didn’t), their “easy money” setups, and the highest-leverage advice they’d give beginners for the next 12 months. It’s pure on process, mindset, and practical how-tos. 

 Huge thank you to Brendan DeGal, MynamesGeff, Xingon, Loopy, Dencave, Mops, Dip, GloMa, Shawn Desjardins, illusionz, Lazyblaze / Wisdom Hunter, and everyone else who shared real processes, journals, charts, and hard-won lessons. This is the kind of practical, no-BS knowledge that actually compounds.
