 A few days ago I shared something regarding a $2M portfolio but it gave some people some concern. 

 Here are the US Stats of the Retirement Portfolio / Liquid Assets (401k, IRA, Roth, etc. — not including home equity) per retiree and how many are in each cohort. 

 Source of info is real data from the Federal Reserve and the Survey of Consumer Finances

 I tried to get data for other countries and the only one I could find was Australia and data was very limited. 9% of Australians have $1M plus which is more than the US (Superannuation balances (ANU / ABS-based 2025 estimates). 

 Some US Stats for Retirees The Millionaire Mark 4.7% or roughly 1 in 20 households have $1M+ 

 Multi-Millionaires 1.8% fewer than 2 in 100 have $2M+ 

 Ultra High Net Worth 0.1% roughly 1 in 1,000 households have $5M+
