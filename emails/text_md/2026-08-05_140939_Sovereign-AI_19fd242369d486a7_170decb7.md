 Gene Munster kinda nailed it. Don’t get distracted by all the noise about how much money SpaceX is spending on Capex and don’t just think of it as “a rocket company that also does AI.”

 Instead, he says SpaceX is the only company in the world that fully owns and controls every piece of its AI system from start to finish. He believes this puts it very early in the process of becoming the most valuable company on the planet.

 Sovereign AI - think end to end hardcore vertical integration They own soup to nuts and more

 The AI model itself (Grok/Cursor)

 The chips that power the AI (they’re working on their own)

 The power generation and megapacks 

 The data centers that train and run the AI

 The network that delivers the AI to users worldwide (Starlink satellites)

 Plus the rockets that can put more of that data center infrastructure into space (2027)

 Rem Unlocks still coming - more downward pressure A total of 911,500,000 shares of SpaceX, stock held by employees and early investors get unlocked tomorrow. SpaceX's free float is set to increase from 4.9% to 11.8% of outstanding shares. That's a whopping 43% more than the 638,900,000 shares issued at IPO. 

 We need to remain patient for this one... price is juicy but trajectory is still down.
