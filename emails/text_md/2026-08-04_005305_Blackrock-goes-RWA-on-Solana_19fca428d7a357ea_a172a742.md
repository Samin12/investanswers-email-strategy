 BlackRock (world’s largest asset manager, ~$15T AUM) is issuing tokenized shares of its new BlackRock Daily Reinvestment Stablecoin Reserve Vehicle (BRSRV) on Solana. This is a regulated government money-market fund that invests only in cash, short-term U.S. Treasuries, and overnight Treasury-backed repos. It’s explicitly designed as an eligible reserve asset for U.S. payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act, with daily dividend reinvestment and multi-chain accessibility.
