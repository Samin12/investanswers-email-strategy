 Why AI is not a bubble A real bubble happens when stock prices go vertical while the actual businesses underneath are still making little or no money. Think late-1990s internet stocks: lots of stories, almost no profits. What’s happening right now is the opposite.

 Companies that are most exposed to AI are not just meeting earnings expectations, they are smashing them. 

 The average S&P 500 company is beating estimates by about 27%

 Nasdaq 100 companies are beating by 55%

 The pure AI companies (chips, data centers, power, networking, memory) are beating by 71%

 When the companies closest to the technology are delivering the strongest earnings growth in decades, it’s hard to call it pure hype.
