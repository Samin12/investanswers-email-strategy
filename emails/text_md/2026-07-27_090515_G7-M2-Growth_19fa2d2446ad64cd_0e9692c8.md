 When M2 grows, it means more money chasing the same number of goods which means higher prices (inflation). Eventually some of this money will also start chasing Bitcoin. 

 The chart shows cumulative growth in broad money supply since January 2004 through May 2026.

 Canada the worst up +368% — by far the highest. Canada’s nominal economy only grew about +159% over the same ~22-year period, so money supply expanded more than twice as fast as economic output which results in crazy debasement. 

 USA second. 

 Japan the best. 

 Oh and Bitcoin is most undervalued against global money supply than any point in history
