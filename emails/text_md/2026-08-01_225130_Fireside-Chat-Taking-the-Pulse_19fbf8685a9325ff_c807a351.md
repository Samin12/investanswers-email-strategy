 July was a tough month for AI stocks. We all felt it. The drawdowns, the narrative shifts, the blowups (yes Leopold). Short-term pain has a way of making everything feel heavier than it is.

 But every single day: people are still winning here in the community.... we want to spread some of that joy eg quiet wins, consistent process, long-term conviction that doesn’t evaporate just because a sector went through a rough patch - so Jacob and I thought this would be a good moment to open the floor and share some of the actual methods by which people in this community win — not the highlight-reel version, but the real stuff. 

 We too will share ours. For me the IA13 saved me in 2026 - as Tesla is not where it should be. 

 The Ask If you’ve had progress (big or small), or if you’ve built a way of operating that keeps you grounded when things get noisy, we’d love for you to share. 

 Answer any or all of these:

 Define what success means to you and how you got there. 

 What does your review/study process look like? 

 Many traders quit before seeing lasting success. Did you have that moment? 

 What is your “easy money” setup? 

 Where would you tell beginners to focus for the next 12 months? (If someone just joined today and asked you for the highest-leverage advice, what would you say?)

 Jacob and I will share ours, drop your answers in the comments. The more specific and honest, the more useful this becomes for everyone else. Together as a community we are stronger.
