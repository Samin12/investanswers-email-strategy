 The table above is how much people spend by age group in retirement and these people have $2M plus on avg. The morale of the story is they can spend a lot more but they do not. Also the older the people get the less they spend. 

 I calculated the safe withdrawal rate given crappy treasury bill type returns mapped back to spending rates by age cohort. 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 eg if you want to retire on $65K a year and u can get 5% return on your money you only need 1.3M to retire. 

 The math changes radically if you are getting 15% a year or 30% a year returns ie you can retire on buttons if you know how to invest... see Retire on Model. 

 Another key point is nobody in retirement actually spends as much as they can. Everyone is afraid of a rainy day and tends to prefer saving. eg a $5M bag you can easily spend $325K a year ie over $27K per month but nodoby does.
