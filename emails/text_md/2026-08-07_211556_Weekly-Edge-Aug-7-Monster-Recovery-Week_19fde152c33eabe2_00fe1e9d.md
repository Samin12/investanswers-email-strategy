 A few key lessons from the week and some sentiment from me - james here...

 DCAS is a killer for layering into IA13 during volatile periods. Set it up correctly: Use the Daily timeframe, set label size to Large , and style to Display . 

 The Broad Market vs. Tech: The S&P 500 is hitting new all-time highs, but tech (especially memory chips like AMD and Micron) is dragging. Money is rotating out of tech and into other areas of the market.

 Marvell (MRVL): The stock is sitting above 200. Do not buy yet. Wait for the price to dip back under 200 and for your tool to flash a buy signal. 

 Tesla (TSLA): Recovering very slowly. SpaceX taking all the limelight. Rem my ratchet.. TSLA should start moving in Lock Step. 

 SpaceX: Wow what a week up $30 or 30%... I was blow away by the earnings call and it looks like I was not the only one. NOTE ME - I am 3/20th loaded on my Position I have the SL $100 Calls and sold the $130 puts.... nicely in profit now but I have another 85% to buy at some stage. I will be patient on this one... no FOMO no POMO. 

 Bitcoin (BTC): We are in a buying zone. Watch $67k and $70k as the next resistance ceilings. If it drops NEAR $50k, treat it as a massive buying opportunity. Even I am buying hard here. 

 Micron (MU): Bank of America has a massive $1,550 price target on the stock. If you are willing to hold through the current bumpy ride, there is significant long-term upside.

 Oil: Watch for prices to drop toward 77. Keep an eye on political news (Trump-related catalysts) that might affect this.

 Palantir (PLTR): Huge week this one is a beast... my BEP is $171 and we are right there but rem w calls you just wait for mean reversion... everything mean reverts 95% of the time.
